# Acceptable Use Policy

Last Updated: 10 August 2026

This Acceptable Use Policy applies to all organisations and users of Samavet ePawati.

This is a draft for product publication. Legal review recommended before production publication.

## 1. Purpose

ePawati is provided for lawful organisation use, including recording Vargani/donation collections, generating digital receipts/Pawati, managing members, maintaining reports, and related organisation workflows.

Users must use ePawati responsibly and only for authorised purposes.

## 2. Prohibited Activities

Users must not:

- Create fake, false, backdated, manipulated, or fraudulent receipts.
- Record donations that were not actually received by the organisation.
- Create or operate fake organisations, fake mandals, fake trusts, or misleading identities.
- Impersonate another person, organisation, trustee, volunteer, collector, donor, or Samavet representative.
- Access or attempt to access another organisation's data without authorisation.
- Share login credentials or allow unauthorised users to use an account.
- Misuse donor names, phone numbers, addresses, collection records, or item donation records.
- Send spam, abusive, misleading, or unauthorised WhatsApp messages.
- Upload malicious files, scripts, malware, illegal content, or unauthorised copyrighted material.
- Attempt to bypass role permissions, tenant restrictions, rate limits, authentication, or security controls.
- Interfere with system reliability, performance, hosting, database, storage, or integrations.
- Scrape, mass-export, sell, or misuse donor data.
- Use ePawati for illegal, deceptive, defamatory, harassing, discriminatory, or harmful activity.
- Use the product to hide, launder, misrepresent, or misuse donation funds.

## 3. Donor Data Protection

Organisations and users must treat donor information carefully. Donor data should be entered only for legitimate organisation recordkeeping, receipt generation, communication, reporting, and compliance purposes.

Users must not use donor data for unrelated marketing, political messaging, personal gain, harassment, sale, or disclosure to unauthorised persons.

## 4. Receipt and Payment Integrity

Because ePawati does not process payments, organisations must independently verify cash, UPI, cheque, bank transfer, or other payment before recording collections as received.

Users must not use ePawati receipts to mislead donors, auditors, members, trustees, banks, government authorities, or the public.

## 5. Uploaded Content

Uploaded content must be lawful, safe, and relevant to the organisation's use of ePawati. This includes logos, receipt/template assets, receipt images, and expense proof images.

Samavet may remove or restrict content that creates security, legal, privacy, or platform risk.

## 6. WhatsApp and Messaging Use

WhatsApp receipt sharing must be used for genuine receipt-related communication. Users must not use ePawati or connected templates for spam, harassment, unrelated promotions, misleading claims, or unauthorised messages.

Organisations are responsible for ensuring donor phone numbers are used lawfully.

## 7. Enforcement

Samavet may investigate suspected violations and may suspend, restrict, or terminate access for serious or repeated violations, subject to applicable law and the Terms and Conditions.

Samavet may also preserve records, cooperate with lawful requests, or take steps needed to protect users, donors, organisations, and the platform.

Legal review recommended before production publication for enforcement, notice, appeal, preservation, and termination wording.
