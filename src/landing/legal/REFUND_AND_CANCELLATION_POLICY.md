# Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: 24 August 2026

This Refund and Cancellation Policy applies to Samavet ePawati subscriptions. 

## 1. Service Type

ePawati is a digital SaaS software product. It does not ship physical products and does not process donation funds.

## 2. Current Standard Plan

The current standard plan supplied for this policy is:

- Customized annual SaaS subscription based on the organisation's requirements, size, usage and features.

If an organisation reaches the 500-slip limit, the organisation must contact Samavet for additional capacity/pricing. Additional capacity is not automatic unless expressly enabled by Samavet.

## 3. Cancellation Before Activation

If an organisation asks to cancel before its account is activated or before receipt capacity is allocated, Samavet may approve cancellation and refund the subscription amount, after deducting any bank charges or taxes if applicable.

The exact treatment should be confirmed in the invoice, proposal, or written communication for that organisation.

## 4. Cancellation After Activation

After the subscription is activated and software access or receipt capacity is allocated, refunds are generally not expected.

This is because ePawati is a digital SaaS subscription and the organisation receives account access, configuration, storage, and annual receipt capacity as part of activation.

However, Samavet may review refund requests in good faith where there is a genuine billing error, duplicate payment, inability to provision the service, or other exceptional circumstance.

## 5. Unused Subscription Time

If an organisation stops using ePawati during the subscription year, unused subscription time is generally not refundable unless Samavet has agreed in writing or applicable law requires otherwise.

## 6. Slip Limit and Additional Capacity

The 500-slip/receipt allocation is part of the annual subscription. Reaching the limit does not by itself create a refund right.

After the limit is reached, the organisation should contact Samavet. Samavet may offer additional capacity, renewal, or revised pricing. Additional slips may be enabled only after the applicable arrangement is completed.

## 7. If Samavet Cannot Provide the Service

If Samavet is unable to provide the subscribed service for reasons within Samavet's control and cannot reasonably restore access within a reasonable time, Samavet may offer one or more of the following:

- Extension of subscription time.
- Service credit.
- Partial refund.
- Full refund in appropriate cases.

The remedy will depend on the nature, duration, and impact of the issue and any applicable law.

## 8. No Refunds for Organisation-side Issues

Refunds are generally not available for:

- Incorrect data entered by the organisation.
- Donor disputes.
- Cash/UPI/payment disputes between donor and organisation.
- WhatsApp delivery issues caused by invalid numbers, template restrictions, third-party provider limits, or WhatsApp platform rules.
- Loss of access caused by organisation user misuse, credential sharing, or policy violations.
- Organisation decision to stop using the software after activation.
