# Donation Disclaimer

Last Updated: 10 August 2026

This Donation Disclaimer explains the responsibility split between Samavet ePawati and organisations using ePawati.

## 1. ePawati Is Not a Payment Processor

Donations/Vargani are transactions directly between the donor and the organisation using ePawati.

ePawati helps the organisation record the collection and generate/share a digital receipt/Pawati. ePawati does not process the underlying payment.
Recording a payment method such as UPI or cash in ePawati does not mean that ePawati verified or processed that payment.

## 2. What Samavet/ePawati Does Not Do

Samavet/ePawati does not:

- Receive donation money.
- Hold donation money.
- Settle donation money.
- Transfer donation money.
- Process UPI transactions.
- Process card transactions.
- Operate a payment wallet.
- Act as a payment gateway.
- Act as a payment aggregator.
- Verify that a donor actually paid the organisation.
- Decide how the organisation uses donations.
- Refund donations to donors.
- Charge a percentage or commission on donations.

## 3. Organisation Responsibility

The organisation is responsible for:

- Confirming receipt of the donation.
- Entering the correct donor name, phone number, address, amount, item details, or other receipt information.
- Selecting the correct payment mode.
- Verifying UPI, cash, cheque, bank transfer, or other payment independently.
- Handling cash and banking controls.
- Maintaining its own accounts and records.
- Correcting receipt mistakes.
- Handling donor questions, donation disputes, and refund requests.
- Deciding and explaining how donations are used.
- Ensuring authorised users do not create fake or inaccurate receipts.

## 4. Donor Understanding

If a donor receives an ePawati receipt, it means the organisation has recorded a collection or item donation in ePawati. The receipt is generated from organisation-entered information.

For payment confirmation, refund requests, receipt corrections, or donation-use questions, donors should contact the relevant organisation directly.

## 5. WhatsApp Receipts

If WhatsApp sharing is enabled, ePawati may help the organisation send or share receipt information through WhatsApp using configured third-party services. WhatsApp message delivery does not mean Samavet received, verified, or processed donation money.

## 6. Legal Review

Legal review recommended before production publication, especially for donor disputes, receipt wording, liability, and payment responsibility.
