## 2. What ePawati Provides

ePawati is SaaS software for mandals, trusts, temples, organisations, societies, and festival committees to digitally manage Vargani/donation collection records, generate receipts/Pawati, manage members/groups, maintain reports, and use related organisation workflows.

ePawati is the software layer. It is not a payment gateway, payment aggregator, wallet, bank, fundraising platform, or financial intermediary.
